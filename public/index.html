          NEW OFFICE
          
    
          
      
      
          An office is an isolated simulation: it has its own missions, its own brain (what it learns stays here) and its own team. It does not read the building's brain or other offices' brains.
          
      
      
      
          AIs that can work here (the office's files only reach these; none checked = all)
        
          
      
          
        
          Employees — characters with their own profile and model; click to open their profile
          
        
            
        
            Logbook — model changes, backups and transfers (written by the server)
            
        
              
      
              
      
      
              
        
              Agent keys — so an external agent can create and follow missions ONLY in this office
              
        
                
        
                
        
                
      
                
      
                
        
                Grok Bots — your Grok bots as employees of this office: they get their turn through their routine's webhook and reply to OfficeMatrix (docs/GROK-BOTS.md)
                
        
                Connection checklist
                
        
                  
        
                  
        
        
                  
          Cloudflare tunnel, for beginners
          
                    
            
                  1. Install cloudflared yourself from Cloudflare's site. OfficeMatrix does not install software and does not need administrator rights.
                    2. 
            
                  2. Quick tunnel (try it): in a terminal, run cloudflared tunnel --url http://localhost:4323. Copy the https address into Public tunnel URL.
                    3. 
            
                  3. Named tunnel (keep it): in Cloudflare Zero Trust, create a tunnel and a public hostname. The service is only http://localhost:4323.
                    4. 
            
                  4. The service must be exactly http://localhost:4323 (the bots' gate). The panel stays on your computer.
                    5. 
            
                  5. Never point the tunnel at port 4321 or at the panel. The gate forwards only bot replies.
                    6.

                     INVITE THE BOT TO YOUR SERVER
                    
        
                    
          Step by step
          
                      
            
                    1. Open discord.com/developers/applications → New Application, named after this office (e.g. ViralHunt).
                      2. 
            
                    2. Bot → Reset Token → copy it and paste it below. Never paste it in a chat.
                      3. 
            
                    3. Save. Then click Invite the bot and choose your server.
                      4. 
            
                    4. In Discord turn on Developer Mode (User Settings → Advanced). Right-click a channel or a person → Copy ID.
                      5. 
            
                    5. Mention the bot once (@bot hi): it answers with your ID. Paste it in Authorized users and save.
                      6. 
            
                    6. Talk to it: @bot @grok find trends about Artemis. Without @name, the office's chat host answers.

                    🏢 NEW SIMULATION · 1/3 BRAND
                    
    
                    
      
                    1. 1 · Brand
                    2. 2 · Employees
                    3. 3 · Review
                    
      
                    
        
                    A simulation is an isolated office: its missions, its brain and its employees. Zero inheritance: it does not read the building's brain or other offices'; whatever you want it to know, you transfer it yourself, entry by entry.
                    
        
                    
          
                    
        
        
        
        
                    
          
          
          
          
          
          
        
                    
      
                    
      
                    
        
                    Each employee is a character: fixed name, job and personality; their model (CLI + model) can change without touching their profile or memory. The backup works for them once if their CLI runs out of credit, hits a limit or loses its session.

                    ↗ TRANSFER

The entry is copied to its incoming tray (outside its brain) and no employee sees it until, inside that office, you press Accept.
                    
      
      
                      
    
                      
                        

  
                      
  
    
                      Getting started
                      
    
                      
      
                      
        
                      ⚙ Settings
                      
        
        The app, the server notices and the briefs for the agents use this language (a request written in another language keeps its own).
      
                      
      
                      1. Install a CLI. 2. Open another terminal and log in with your subscription. 3. Restart the server and press Refresh.
                      
      
                      Test creates a one-turn mission in the building and uses some of your subscription quota. You will see the answer or the error in its record.
