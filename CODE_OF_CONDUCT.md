# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct / Código de Conducta

## Our Pledge (English)

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

### Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our community include:
* Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward others.
* Being respectful of differing opinions and viewpoints (debate with evidence, never attack the person).
* Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
* Never publishing private information, credentials, or session tokens.

---

## Nuestro Compromiso (Español)

Como miembros, colaboradores y líderes, nos comprometemos a hacer que la participación en nuestra comunidad sea una experiencia libre de acoso para todo el mundo.

### Nuestros Estándares

Ejemplos de comportamiento constructivo:
* Demostrar empatía y amabilidad con los demás.
* Debatir con argumentos y datos verificados, nunca con ataques personales.
* Aceptar retroalimentación constructiva.
* No compartir nunca claves, credenciales o datos privados.
